How It Works

STEP 01

Drop or Search

Upload any PDF, invoice, receipt, contract, or image directly. Alternatively, describe what you need in plain language to find existing files.

STEP 02

Intelligent Classification

Dual-stage analysis evaluates structural keywords, entity patterns, and layout features to assign a calibrated confidence score between 0% and 100%.

STEP 03

Instant Filing & Safety

Documents with 85% confidence or higher file immediately into structured folders. Ambiguous items route safely to the Review Queue or Awaiting Approval.

Core Capabilities

Calibrated Confidence

Dual-layer scoring blends structural regex indicators with AI semantic verification to prevent false filings.

Controlled Folder Approvals

When unfamiliar categories emerge, DocPilot holds documents in Awaiting Approval until you confirm directory creation.

Automated Folder Watchers

Background directory monitoring tracks Downloads and inbox folders, catching incoming files without manual intervention.

Descriptive Natural Search

Can't recall the exact filename? Search by vendor, date, total amount, or agreement clauses with instant sqlite indexing.
